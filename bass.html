Mic level

Play one note at a time and let it ring. The note lights up in one spot on the neck, the one nearest the last note you played, green when it's in the scale and red when it isn't. The needle shows how far off pitch it is, so this works as a tuner too. Low notes take a moment to read; a pickup or amp into an interface reads better than the phone's mic.
