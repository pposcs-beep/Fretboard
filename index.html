Tap a dot to hear

Pick one shape to outline it, or two next to each other to join them into one bigger box, like C and A, or E and D. Full shows every note of the scale. Play lick lights up each note on the neck and in the tab under it as it plays. With Listen on, the first tab note lights up and moves on each time you play the right one, so you can work through the lick at your own speed. Listen to my guitar also follows your lead lines: each note you play lights up where it sits, blue in the shape, gold in the scale but outside the shape, and red when it isn't in the scale. It follows single notes; chords won't show right. The key is the same key you pick at the top of the page.

My Songs

Photo of the chart

Type the chords as you'd write them, like G C/E Em7 Dsus4 G13. Section words like Verse: are fine. Each chord gets a picture; harder ones also show an easier swap. Tap a picture to see that chord on the neck. Type the key and each chord also shows its number, like 1, 4, 5; with no key, the first chord counts as home. Add photo keeps a picture of the chart with the song, from the camera or your photos. Songs save in this browser on this device, so use Export songs now and then to keep a backup.

Open a tab saved as a PDF or a text file, or paste one in. Play lights each note on the neck and in the tab as you hear it; tap any note in the tab to hear it and start from there. Speed is beats per minute. Turn on Listen to my guitar and play along: each note you play shows as right or off. With Wait for me on, the tab waits for you to play each note before it moves on, so you can go at your own pace. Tab that's only a scanned picture can't be read.
